Agronomic Impact of Drip Irrigation

Adoption expanded from 21.0% in 2005 to over 82.5% in 2025. Drip irrigation delivers balanced soluble fertilizers directly to root zones, conserves 45% water compared to flood basins, and drove palm productivity gains of 50.0%.

Microsoft Power BI Report Integration Active

This application synchronizes its dataset (data/pollachi_datasets.csv) with Microsoft Power BI Desktop. Interactive DAX measures (Total Yield, Copra Mandi CAGR, and Drip Fertigation Efficiency) are modeled and embedded into this portal.

📥 Download Power BI Data Source (CSV)
Power BI Data Modeling & DAX Calculation Measures:
1. Production Growth (DAX):
Yield_CAGR = DIVIDE(MAX('Data'[Production_Cr]) - MIN('Data'[Production_Cr]), MIN('Data'[Production_Cr]))
2. Mandi Price Realization (DAX):
Avg_Copra_Price = AVERAGE('Data'[Copra_Price_Qtl])
3. Water Savings Index (DAX):
Water_Cons_Mld = SUM('Data'[Area_Ha]) * AVERAGE('Data'[Drip_Pct]) * 0.45
